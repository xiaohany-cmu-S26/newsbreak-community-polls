Two arrested after armed robbery at Mountain View gas station
Mountain View police arrested two men early Tuesday after an armed robbery at a gas station on El Camino Real, authorities said.

Officers responded to a report of a robbery at about 1:40 a.m. The clerk told police that two men entered the store, one displaying a handgun, and demanded cash from the register before fleeing in a dark sedan. No one was injured.

Patrol officers located a vehicle matching the description on Highway 101 near Rengstorff Avenue about 20 minutes later. The driver and passenger were taken into custody without incident, and a replica firearm was recovered from the vehicle, police said.

The suspects, ages 24 and 19, were booked into Santa Clara County jail on suspicion of robbery and conspiracy. Police asked anyone with information to contact the department's investigations unit.
